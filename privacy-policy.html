YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Learn how HavenGH collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to HavenGH. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information when you use our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, how we protect it and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use HavenGH, we may collect information including:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account login information
  • Property information
  • Rental requests
  • Saved properties
  • Messages between users
  • Profile information
  • Information you provide when contacting us

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your HavenGH account.
  • Allow tenants to search for and save properties.
  • Allow tenants to send rental requests.
  • Allow tenants and agents to communicate.
  • Allow agents and landlords to list and manage properties.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve the HavenGH website and services.
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent or unauthorised activity.

4. Property and Contact Information

Agents and landlords may provide property information for publication on HavenGH.

Some information submitted by agents or landlords may be displayed publicly so that potential tenants can view properties and contact the relevant agent or landlord.

Users should therefore avoid submitting sensitive personal information in public property descriptions.

5. Messages and Rental Requests

When a tenant sends a rental request or message to an agent or landlord, information necessary to provide that communication service may be shared with the intended recipient.

HavenGH may retain these communications to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes and improve the platform.

6. Account Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their passwords confidential and notify HavenGH if they believe their account has been accessed without permission.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared where necessary to provide a service, where the user has requested or authorised the sharing, or where disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law.

8. Data Retention

We will retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect our legitimate interests.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights concerning their personal information, including rights to access, correct or object to certain processing of their information.

Ghana's Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843) provides data subjects with rights relating to their personal information. ([Data Protection Ghana](https://dataprotection.org.gh/for-individuals/?utm))

10. Cookies and Local Storage

HavenGH may use cookies, browser storage or similar technologies to support login sessions, preferences, saved properties and website functionality.

As the platform develops, this policy may be updated to describe additional technologies used by HavenGH.

11. Children's Privacy

HavenGH is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is not permitted by applicable law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as HavenGH develops or as legal and regulatory requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with the relevant update date.

13. Contact HavenGH

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact HavenGH through the contact details provided on our website.

We will review privacy-related requests and respond in accordance with applicable requirements.

14. Data Protection in Ghana

HavenGH intends to handle personal information in accordance with applicable Ghanaian data-protection requirements, including the Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843).

The Data Protection Commission is the statutory body responsible for enforcing Ghana's data protection framework. ([Data Protection Ghana](https://dataprotection.org.gh))
